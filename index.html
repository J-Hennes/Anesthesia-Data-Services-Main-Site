Our Solution

The heart of the ADS platform is a dynamic scheduling process that allows facilities to request their specific coverage needs on specific days. Providers can be assigned, work information can be captured, and provider pay and facility invoices can all be easily calculated. The platform allows for a collaboration that DRIVES EFFECIENCES for groups and facilities.
